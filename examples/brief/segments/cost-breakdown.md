Monthly platform spend by component, rounded to the nearest whole unit. Compute
is billed per allocated core hour and storage per provisioned gigabyte, so an
idle cluster still accrues both. Egress is billed per gigabyte leaving the
region and is the only line item that varies with request volume rather than
with allocation.

| egress | 0.19 | rising sharply |
| other | 0.05 | flat |
